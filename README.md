### Projects

**Analysis of the tritium beta-decay spectrum in KATRIN to set limits on sterile-neutrino parameters** — *Thesis (in progress)*<br>
Advisor: Víctor Saúl Basto González, Ph.D. · Universidad del Quindío

Statistical analysis of the public data from the first KATRIN science run (KNM1) to test the eV-scale sterile-neutrino hypothesis suggested by the reactor and gallium anomalies, independently of reactor fluxes.

- Theoretical background: massive neutrinos beyond the Standard Model, lepton mixing and the 3+1 scheme; the tritium beta spectrum from Fermi theory, including the Fermi function, the molecular final-state distribution of T<sub>2</sub> and the KATRIN MAC-E filter.
- Fortran 2008 code developed together with my advisor: χ² fit with a pull term and profiling of nuisance parameters. Result: *m*<sub>β</sub><sup>2</sup> = −1.15 ± 0.81 eV² (68% CL), consistent with the value published by KATRIN.
- Exclusion contours in the (sin²2θ<sub>ee</sub>, Δ*m*²<sub>41</sub>) plane for the 3+1 scenario, with confidence levels given by Wilks' theorem, compared with the results of Giunti, Li and Zhang (2019).
