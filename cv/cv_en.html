Projects

Analysis of the tritium beta-decay spectrum in KATRIN to set limits on sterile-neutrino parameters · Thesis (in progress)
Advisor: Víctor Saúl Basto González, Ph.D. · Universidad del Quindío
Statistical analysis of the public data from the first KATRIN science run (KNM1) to test the eV-scale sterile-neutrino hypothesis suggested by the reactor and gallium anomalies, independently of reactor fluxes.
  • Theoretical background: massive neutrinos beyond the Standard Model, lepton mixing and the 3+1 scheme; the tritium beta spectrum from Fermi theory, including the Fermi function, the molecular final-state distribution of T2 and the KATRIN MAC-E filter.
  • Fortran 2008 code developed together with my advisor: χ2 fit with a pull term and profiling of nuisance parameters. Result: mβ2 = −1.15 ± 0.81 eV2 (68% CL), consistent with the value published by KATRIN.
  • Exclusion contours in the (sin22θee, Δm241) plane for the 3+1 scenario, with confidence levels given by Wilks' theorem, compared with the results of Giunti, Li and Zhang (2019).
